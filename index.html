About-us

Bridging research, technology and real-world impact.

We are a deep-tech innovation and research company specialising in statistics, data analytics, artificial intelligence, agentic AI, quantum computing, bioinformatics and AI-driven automation. Founded in 2025, we bridge research, technology and real-world impact.

Our mission is to empower businesses, researchers and students with advanced analytical capabilities, while building the next generation of technology talent through training, collaboration and applied research.

  • Publication-grade research rigour with deployable engineering
  • End-to-end delivery, from consultation to support
  • Multi-domain experience across agriculture, health and finance

Flagship product: deployed

5eriMitra AI

A smart sericulture assistant for farmers, extension officers and silk-industry researchers. It combines a chat agent, voice calling and a curated knowledge base to deliver expert guidance in the field, in the user's own language.

  • Chat

    Ask questions and get expert guidance.

  • Voice

    Voice calling as an alternative to typing.

  • Vision

    Upload a silkworm photo for grasserie, instar and symptom analysis.

  • Regional intelligence

    Mulberry cultivation, rearing schedules, 4th-instar care and location advisories.

  • Multilingual

    Native-language responses by location.
