Questions

Good to know

Do I need any programming background?

No. The programme starts from the very basics and everything runs in the browser through Google Colab.

What do I need to attend?

A laptop or desktop with a stable internet connection, and a Google account for Colab notebooks.

What if I miss a session?

All sessions are recorded. Recordings stay available for 3 months after the programme.

Which fee applies to me?

The student fee (₹1,499) applies to UG, PG and PhD students and research scholars. Faculty, scientists, industry and all other participants pay the professional fee (₹1,999). Both include GST.

Which UPI apps can I pay with?

Any UPI app: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank's app. On a phone, the Pay now button opens the app directly; on a computer, scan the QR code with your phone. You can also pay to UPI ID 9480363456@okbizaxis.

When will I get the joining link?

Once your payment is verified, joining details are shared by email and WhatsApp before the programme begins on 16 November 2026.
